# Ambience

Background loops: rain, a crowded tavern, wind in a cave. They loop without a seam and play under the music.

A folder here starts with the Music playlist of the **same name**: `Ambience/Tavern` plays under `Music/01 Tavern`. Folders with no matching playlist (like Weather) are layers you start by hand, from the Sound library or the Console, or add to any playlist on the screen.

# Ambience

Each folder here is an **ambience playlist** on the DM screen: rain, a crowded tavern, wind in a cave. Its files play one after another in file-name order and the playlist repeats. It plays **under** the music and the soundboard, so a scene's music, its ambience and the effects all play at once.

A folder starts by itself with the Music playlist of the **same name**: `Ambience/Tavern` plays under `Music/01 Tavern`. Any of them can also be started on its own from the Playlists pane or the Console, or with its number key (playlists take Alt+1 to 9 in folder order, music first). Starting another ambience playlist takes the place of the one playing.

To add one, make a folder: on the screen, Playlists, **New** beside Ambience. A music playlist's **⋯** menu can also make it an ambience playlist, which moves its folder here.

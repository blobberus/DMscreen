# Towns & Cities

The **Towns & Cities** ambience playlist. Its files play one after another in file-name order, under the music and the soundboard, and it starts by itself with the **Towns & Cities** music playlist (the folder in sounds/Music with the same name). Its number key is set on the screen.

**To add sounds:** choose **Add file**, then **Upload files**, drop in your MP3s (up to 25 MB each) and press **Commit changes**. They show up on the DM screen about a minute later.

Naming rules and the rest of the folders: [sounds/README.md](../../README.md)

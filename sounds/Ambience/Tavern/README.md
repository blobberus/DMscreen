# Tavern

The **Tavern** ambience playlist. Its files play one after another in file-name order, like any playlist: starting it ends what was playing. To play one of its sounds over something else, use that sound's on-top button in the Playlists pane. Its number key is set on the screen.

**To add sounds:** choose **Add file**, then **Upload files**, drop in your MP3s (up to 25 MB each) and press **Commit changes**. They show up on the DM screen about a minute later.

Naming rules and the rest of the folders: [sounds/README.md](../../README.md)
